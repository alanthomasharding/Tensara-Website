# Tensara brand foundations

Working draft, derived from the 2026.10.07 corporate overview deck. Use it to keep the website, decks and outreach consistent.

## Positioning

**For** reconstructive and pelvic-floor surgeons and the patients they treat,
**who** are stuck choosing between permanent plastic mesh and a second surgery to harvest the patient's own tissue,
**Tensara** is a resorbable nanofiber scaffold
**that** guides the body to rebuild organized, functional tissue — then dissolves.
**Unlike** permanent polypropylene mesh or current macroporous resorbables,
**Tensara** controls tissue architecture at the fiber level and leaves no permanent foreign body behind.

**One-liner:** Next-generation soft tissue repair in women's health and beyond.
**Short form:** Restore native tissue — don't replace it.

## Pillars

1. **Restore, don't replace** — the scaffold directs regeneration, then resorbs.
2. **Engineered at the nanoscale** — coaxial electrospun nanofibers, aligned to mimic native tissue.
3. **One platform, many tissues** — a tunable platform; soft tissue first, more indications to follow.
4. **Built on a clear path** — lead with the indication that has the clearest regulatory precedent.

## Voice

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Precise — say what the fibers do | Hype-driven ("revolutionary", "cure") |
| Clinically grounded — talk like a surgeon would | Jargon for its own sake |
| Confident but honest about stage | Implying clearance, availability, or proven outcomes |
| Patient-aware — women's health is personal | Clinical-cold |

Rules for public copy:
- Use "designed to", "developed to", "aims to" for product benefits. Never claim outcomes not yet shown in studies.
- Always pair product claims with the investigational status line.
- Every statistic needs a source before it goes live.
- Keep financials, round terms, license terms and runway out of public materials.

## Visual identity

| Ink | `#222831` | Body text |

| Sage | `#789C8C` | Supporting accents |

| Mist | `#F7F8FA` | Alternate section background |

**Type:** Figtree (headings) + Noto Sans (body). Noto Sans matches the deck; Figtree was the UI UX Pro Max recommendation for healthcare.

**Imagery:** real lab and micrograph imagery over stock photos. Confirm rights for any figure taken from literature or illustration tools (e.g. BioRender) before publishing.
